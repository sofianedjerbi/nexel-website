Nexel Worlds

A cozy top-down survival game in a world of blocks. Dig down, build up, farm, and stay out of the dark.

{{icon:play}}Play in your browser

Free, nothing to install, works on a phone. Your world is saved in your browser.

What it looks like

A corner of the shared world: two houses players built block by block.

Walk into a hill and the rock opens over you: you always see where you are.

Night. Bring a torch; fires light the way home.

A river down a stone gorge. Water is see-through, so you can read its depth.

Water flows. Cut a river's bank and it fills your pit, a layer at a time.

Build mode: plan a row with the cursor, and your character builds it, block by block.

What you do

  • Chop, mine and dig: the land goes 512 blocks down and 512 up.
  • Build houses block by block: walls, floors, stairs, ladders, doors and roofs.
  • Farm, keep cows and chickens, cook, craft from a recipe book.
  • Explore caves, gorges, rivers and mountains, by day and by night.
  • Meet the locals: some are friendly, some are not.
  • Share a world with friends, or with AI players that run on a local model.

Controls

WASDWalk (or the arrows). Shift sprints SpaceJump EUse: open, place, eat QBreak: mine, chop, hit (or left click) 1-9What is in your hand I CInventory, crafting ZBuild mode EnterTalk HHelp (all the keys)

Code

The game and its AI players are open on GitHub.

  • nexel-worlds: the game (server in Python, client in plain JavaScript).
  • nexel-agent: the AI players, from a local language model to reinforcement learning.

The demo runs the whole game server inside your browser tab.
