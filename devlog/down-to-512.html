Floors, tunnels, and 512 blocks down

Houses got storeys: stairs and ladders, floors you walk on and build on, a roof as a block like the rest.

A house of three storeys in the flat look, 16 November

One view when you are hidden

Digging a stair under a hill showed the next problem: the cave had one way of being drawn, a hollow under the meadow another, and the stair crossed from one to the other. The feedback was clear: everything should have the same view, not one view per situation. Since then there is exactly one rule for when you are hidden, whatever hides you.

A stair dug under a hill, 17 November

512 down, 512 up

The world was 32 cells tall: digging stopped at -12. A column is now stored as one number with a bit per cell, as long as it needs to be, from -512 to 512. Old saves were backed up first and carried over.

The AI players get bodies

The AI players are robots: each race in steel, so you can tell them apart from people at a glance.
