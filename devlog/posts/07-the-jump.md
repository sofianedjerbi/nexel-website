Jumping is the smallest thing in a game and it took the longest.

The first hop (back on the 12th) looked wrong twice. The first rewrite was judged on poses placed by hand: a run-up, a crouch, an arc, a landing. Recorded frame by frame in the real browser, it popped up, hung in the air, and popped down. From then on, no animation counts as better until we have looked at a recording of real play.

## Blocks change everything

When the world became full cubes, a step became a block high, and the old hop was not enough. The steps:

- No automatic step-up; then automatic jumping as a setting, on by default.
- A drawn pose for each moment: crouch, push off, tuck, top, fall, land, settle.
- A real jump through the air, keeping your speed, instead of a short teleport.
- No jumping through a roof; a refused jump keeps your walk going.
- The camera follows you through the jump; only what is left over eases.
- In the air, the view takes your feet's level.
