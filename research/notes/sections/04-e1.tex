\section*{E1. One learning serves every weighting}
\begin{theorem}[successor features] Let $\psi^\pi(s,a)=\mathbb E^\pi[\sum_t\gamma^t\phi(s_t,a_t)\mid s_0=s,a_0=a]$. Then
$Q^\pi_w=\psi^{\pi\top}w$ for every $w$.\end{theorem}
\begin{proof} $Q^\pi_w(s,a)=\mathbb E^\pi[\sum_t\gamma^t\phi(s_t,a_t)^\top w]=\psi^\pi(s,a)^\top w$ by linearity of
expectation.\end{proof}
\noindent Check. 20 Garnets, 50 random $w$ each: largest $|\psi^\top w-Q_w|=2.1\times10^{-14}$. Learned by TD from samples
(step $1/n^{0.7}$, so $\sum$ steps $=\infty$, $\sum$ squares $<\infty$), the relative error falls to $4.2\%$
(IQM, 95\% CI $[4.0, 4.3]$) after $3\times10^5$ samples (Figure E1).

\emph{So:} the mind may set $w$ (how much it cares for each kind) and the body has the value of that mix at once.
