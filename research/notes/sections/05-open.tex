\section*{Next, and open questions}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The body as skills.} Retrain it as a few short skills (go to, gather an item, fight, flee), each rewarded by
shaping on progress the game verifies, combined by GPI or one network conditioned on the weighting [USFA, Borsa et al.\ 2019], the base
skills chosen by SFOLS [Alegre et al.\ 2022]. Measure: goals done per hour, in the simulator, against the current body.
\item \textbf{Order of construction.} Plan by disassembly: take cells off the finished design, each one reachable and
leaving the rest standing, and lay them in the reverse order. The tower is the test.
\item \textbf{Splitting a shared network.} Not by the sign of a gradient cosine (revision 2's E3: at a minimum of a sum
of two losses the gradients are always opposite, so it detects convergence, not conflict). Measure conflict during
training, as PCGrad does [Yu et al.\ 2020], and split where a split's measured loss gain is worth its parameters.
\item \textbf{Evaluation.} More seeds per design, the same seeds for the bodies compared (paired), an interval on every
rate; the simulator's completion rate against the live one, once live trials of the executor exist.
\end{enumerate}
