\section*{Next, and open questions}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The learned body.} Its role is now reflexes and what no skill can step. Retraining it as a few short
skills conditioned on a reward weighting [USFA, Borsa et al.\ 2019], the skills chosen so that combining them by
generalised policy improvement [Barreto et al.\ 2017] is optimal for every weighting [SFOLS, Alegre et al.\ 2022],
each rewarded by shaping on progress the game verifies [Ng et al.\ 1999]; measured on the goal table above.
\item \textbf{The language model under load.} In trial 7 its requests failed 75 times; the executor carried on, the
second house did not start. Requests that outlive the server's queue should be dropped server-side, not only by the
client.
\item \textbf{The last three stalls.} Seed 21 twice (a roof cell, a tower wall) and the T on seed 7: each a run of
decisions with no piece laid, to be traced as the six faults above were.
\item \textbf{Evaluation.} Live trials cost an hour of a shared machine: the simulator boards are the measure, the
live trial their check; more seeds, paired between versions, an interval on every rate.
\end{enumerate}
